Hackeriet 3D model

An interactive 3D scan of Hackeriet, a hackerspace in Oslo.

After enabling navigation: W/S move forward/back, A/D move left/right, R/F move up/down. Arrow keys mirror WASD, PageUp/PageDown mirror R/F, mouse wheel changes movement speed, left-drag looks around, right-drag translates, and double-click jumps toward a point.
